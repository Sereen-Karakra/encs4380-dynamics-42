1 · Plant model

Drive motor: speed = G(s) · PWM

G(s) = K / (τs + 1)  |  input: PWM command (0–100 %)  |  output: wheel speed measured by the encoder

Estimate K and τ from a PWM step test

Apply a constant PWM step, log the encoder speed, then enter: the PWM level, the steady speed reached, and the time when the speed first reaches 63.2 % of that steady speed. K = steady speed / PWM, τ = that time.

What the speed loop must do

How fast the speed must settle after a command.

Fixed by the homework: damping ratio 0.6 ≤ ζ ≤ 0.8 and PWM command inside 0–100 %. The target must also be reachable: r ≤ K · 100 %.

3 · Design

Choose ζ and ωn, get Kp and Ki

PI on a first-order plant: ωn = √(K·Ki/τ)  |  ζ = (1 + K·Kp) / (2√(τ·K·Ki))
Design: Kp = (2ζωnτ − 1)/K  |  Ki = ωn²τ/K

Method: PI design by matching the closed-loop characteristic polynomial τs² + (1 + K·Kp)s + K·Ki to s² + 2ζωn s + ωn² (scaled). The solver is our own RK4 implementation, the same one used in the PID Playground.
