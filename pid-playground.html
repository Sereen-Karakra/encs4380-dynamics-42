02

Closed-loop block diagram

The measured output returns to the summing junction, where it is subtracted from the setpoint.

u(t) = clip(Kp·e(t) + Ki·∫e(t)dt − Kd·dy(t)/dt, −Umax, +Umax)

Derivative is taken from the measured output to avoid a derivative kick when the setpoint steps. The plant receives the saturated signal; integration is continuous (RK4). P1 does not include the P3 anti-windup switch.
